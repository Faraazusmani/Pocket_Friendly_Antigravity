# Multi-Currency

User selects a default currency during setup and may change it later.

Historical transactions retain their original currency. Changing default currency affects future data only.

When changing from INR to USD, offer a quick path to create a new USD account.

Do not use external exchange rates or silently convert historical records. Do not combine unrelated currencies in calculations.

Dashboard, budgets, Safe-to-Spend and repayment warnings are currency-scoped and shown in per-currency sections.
